About this prototype

This is a keyboard-first web app shell, not a replacement Android operating system.

It can navigate its own menus and mini apps when the browser receives hardware key events.

It cannot make native Android apps keypad-compatible. A separate Android app with a WebView or an Accessibility Service would be needed for deeper integration.
